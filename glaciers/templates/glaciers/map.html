about

Geometric centrelines of each glacier's main branch and major tributaries, RGI 7.0 Consortium (2023), computed with OGGM (Maussion and others 2019) by the method of Kienholz and others (2014). doi:10.5067/F6JMOVY5NAVZ. They stop at each glacier's outline and at its present terminus.

The brighter blue lines are the network being built here: RGI lines taken in, joined across glaciers and drawn on down-valley past today's ice. A dot marks the downstream end of each (solid = an outlet, ring = joins another line); an orange dot marks a fork, where a distributary leaves its parent for a second front. Click a network line to plot ice-face positions against distance along it.
