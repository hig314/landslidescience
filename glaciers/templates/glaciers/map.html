about

Geometric centrelines of each glacier's main branch and major tributaries, RGI 7.0 Consortium (2023), computed with OGGM (Maussion and others 2019) by the method of Kienholz and others (2014). doi:10.5067/F6JMOVY5NAVZ. They stop at each glacier's outline and at its present terminus.

The brighter blue lines are the network being built here: RGI lines taken in, joined across glaciers and drawn on down-valley past today's ice. A dot marks the downstream end of each (solid = an outlet, ring = joins another line). Click a network line to plot ice-face positions against distance along it.

Editing the network
Click a line to select it. Then: drag a vertex to move it; drag a midpoint to add one; click a vertex and press Delete (or right-click it) to remove it, and keep pressing to remove the next; Ctrl/⌘-Z undoes. Nothing is drawn until you press a ＋ button. Joining: drag the end of a flow line onto another and it snaps; sharing that point is the join. End on the middle of a line to make a feeder; end on another line's start and the two become one line.
Click a vertex, then Delete removes it. The + at each end of the line extends it from that end.
Editors: the ✎ button on the map opens the network for editing.
