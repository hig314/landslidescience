In raw, every mark is one ITS_LIVE Level-2 measurement: a displacement over its own image-pair interval, nothing gridded or fitted. Filtered hides what the robust fit rejected; rejected only shows exactly that discarded population, so the cleaning can be inspected rather than trusted. Fitted leaves the measurements behind and samples the model itself — including cells that exist only by extrapolation from the longer record or by spatial fill, which are drawn dimmer. A measurement is drawn ending at its grid node and reaching back v·dt upstream (the published product does not carry the exact chip offset). Level-2 data is unfiltered — the near-stationary haze at long separations is documented “skipping/locking”.
