Difference

Colours the overlap of two surveys by how fast the ground moved between them, ColorBrewer PRGn: green where it rose, purple where it fell, neutral where it did not, transparent outside the overlap. Two surveys years apart are scaled as a RATE, metres per year, so a pair eleven years apart and a pair one year apart can be read against each other. Two renderings of the same date are scaled in plain metres. A whole survey in one flat colour is not change but a vertical datum disagreement, which is what this is best at catching.

3D

Without context a survey stands on a flat plane as a block of towers. Context is USGS 3DEP, fetched live per tile (slow on first view); in Alaska that is 2010 IfSAR, so retreated glaciers still show as ice and the survey steps off them with a cliff. It fills only around the survey: water and other voids inside one stay voids, at 0 m, rather than being painted with a regional DEM's guess at the sea surface. The fog spares the renderer the far field, and applies only once the view is pitched: looking straight down there is no far field, and on a deep bathymetric survey the fog reads depth as distance and washes the seabed out past a certain contour.
