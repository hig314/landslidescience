Photo drops

A drop is a link you send someone so they can upload a folder of photos. Files go straight from their browser to private object storage (never through this server), anyone with a site account can browse what arrived, and tools/drops/r2_pull.sh <slug> mirrors a drop to Nunatak for processing and backup.
